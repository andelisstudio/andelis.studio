Small puzzle games for iPhone, engineered with care.

One person, one game at a time, no compromises on the logic.

Andelis Studio makes small puzzle games for iPhone and takes the engineering seriously: every board is checked before it reaches you, every hint is honest, and nothing about you is collected. The first game is Stelline.

Games

  • Stelline

    iPhone · iOS 26 or later · one purchase

    Stelline is a calm star-placement logic game: put one queen, or two stars, in every row, column and region of the board, and never let two touch. Every board is generated on your phone, verified to have exactly one solution, and graded by the same deduction engine that powers its hints.
